Sebastian Frånlund Johansson

PhD candidate in Economics, BI Norwegian Business School
Senior analyst, Menon Economics

I am a labor economist. My research uses Norwegian administrative registers to study how workers and firms adjust to shocks: the 2014 oil-price collapse, mass layoffs in high-premium industries, and the spread of artificial intelligence across occupations. I am interested in job displacement, social insurance, occupational mobility and the networks that connect occupations.

Before BI, I worked as an economist at the Swedish Competition Authority (Konkurrensverket). I hold a master's and a bachelor's degree in economics from the University of Gothenburg.

Artificial Intelligence, Worker Reallocation, and Occupational Networks

A complementarity-adjusted AI exposure index linked to Norwegian employer–employee registers and an occupational mobility network. Exposure lowers within-firm employment only where task complementarity is low.

AI exposure explorer

Each point is one of 422 four-digit ISCO-08 occupations. The horizontal axis is the Dynamic AI Occupational Exposure index (DAIOE, all applications, extended to 2024); the vertical axis is the task-complementarity index, which measures how much the occupation's tasks sit in contexts where human input remains valuable. Dashed lines mark the medians (35.0 and 0.32). The complementarity-adjusted index C-DAIOE used in my job market paper discounts exposure by complementarity, so the occupations where AI is most likely to substitute for labor are those in the lower-right quadrant.

About the data

DAIOE is the Dynamic AI Occupational Exposure index of Engberg, Görg, Lodefalk et al., which combines AI benchmark progress across applications with the abilities required in each occupation. The published series ends in 2023; the 2024 values extend it using benchmark progress from the Papers with Code archive, as described in the paper's appendix. The complementarity index follows the task-context approach of Pizzinelli et al. (2023). Scores are shown for the 422 occupations with Norwegian employment in the paper's sample; armed forces occupations are excluded. The underlying file is data/exposure_2024.json.
